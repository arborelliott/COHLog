Combat Log Analyzer

Paste a City of Heroes chat log, or drop the .txt file in, to see what each of your characters did: damage and DPS by power, healing, endurance, control, buffs and debuffs, defeats and rewards, and how long you played. Everything runs in your browser; your log is never uploaded.

Your log

Turn logging on in game with /logchat. Logs are saved under Homecoming\accounts\<account>\Logs. Each “Welcome to City of Heroes” line starts a new character session. Gaps between your actions longer than the idle gap count as downtime, not combat.

What’s counted

  • A character’s session runs from its “Welcome to City of Heroes” line until the next character logs in or the log ends. Several sessions for the same character are combined in one tab.
  • Combat time counts stretches where you were acting: damaging, healing, controlling, buffing or debuffing. Gaps longer than the idle gap are left out.
  • Only effects your log attributes to you are counted. Pet damage, such as Fire Imps, isn’t written to your log. Healing and buffs you receive from teammates aren’t counted as yours.
  • “Self healing” includes inspirations such as Respite and Awaken. “Endurance gained” comes from lines like “Your Take a Breather grants you…”; a few teammate powers are worded the same way in the log, so this figure can include some of theirs.
  • Control counts each time a hold, immobilize, stun, sleep or knockdown landed on a target. “Overpower” counts the extra magnitude applications from the Overpower effect.
  • Casts come from “You activated” lines; accuracy comes from HIT and MISSED roll lines.
